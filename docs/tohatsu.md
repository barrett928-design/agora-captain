## Critical Operating Notes
- **Flush after every salt water use** — run engine in fresh water bucket for several minutes to clear salt from cooling passages
- **Do not run dry** — impeller fails quickly without water
- **Tilt fully** when trailering or storing — drains water from cooling passages
- **Fuel** — use fresh gasoline; ethanol blends above E10 can degrade fuel lines and carb components
- **Break-in** — vary throttle, avoid sustained full throttle for first 10 hours; change oil at 20 hours
