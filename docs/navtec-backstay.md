# Navtec Hydraulic Backstay Adjuster

Hydraulic ram with a built-in hand pump that tensions the backstay.
**Model number:** not yet recorded. Read it off the cylinder or pump body and add it here.

## Hydraulic fluid
- **Navtec spec:** clean, non-detergent hydraulic oil, **ISO VG 15**
  - Shell Tellus T15 (what Navtec uses), Mobil DTE 11M, Chevron Rando HDZ 15, Royal Purple Syndraulic 15
- **Common alternative:** ISO VG 32 (easier to find; fine in warm climates). Don't mix types; if switching, drain and refill
- **Never use brake fluid.** It attacks the seals
- Sources: Navtec Hydraulics FAQ (navtechydraulics.com/faqs); Navtec Series 9 Integral manual

## Rod care (monthly in season)
1. Rinse the chrome rod with fresh water
2. Wipe clean with a soft cloth
3. Check for pitting, rust, scratches, or oil weeping at the gland (where the rod enters the cylinder)
4. Wipe on a thin film of the same hydraulic oil, cycle the rod in and out, and wipe off the excess
- **Don't use** 3-in-1 (collects grit, which the wiper drags into the seals), WD-40 (solvent that dries seals), or grease

## Annual check (backstay fully eased, gauge at zero)
- Put a few drops of light oil on the handle pivot pins and linkage (3-in-1 is fine *here*)
- Clean the vent/fill plug. A blocked vent creates a vacuum and makes the pump hard to work
- Check the fluid level and color. It should be clear and light; dark, milky, or gritty fluid means flush and refill

## Troubleshooting: hard to pump
| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Gets heavy only as the gauge nears max | Normal |
| Hard from the first stroke, backstay eased | Corroded handle pivots, blocked vent, low or dirty fluid, salt-crusted rod |
| Easier with the fill plug loosened 1/4 turn | Blocked vent: clean the vent hole |
| Still hard after the checks above | Sticky internal pump valves or worn seals: rigging shop |
| Oil on the rod, or pressure drops overnight | Seal leak: rebuild needed (rigging shop) |

Navtec's original company no longer makes these. Seal kits may need to be sourced or cross-referenced by a hydraulics/rigging shop.

## Safety
- Only open the fill plug or any fitting with the backstay fully eased and the gauge at zero
- Never pump past the maximum pressure marked on the gauge or cylinder
- For seal work, support the mast separately (e.g. main halyard led aft to a strong point)
