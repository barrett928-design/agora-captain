## DSC Distress Call Procedure

⚠️ **GPS must be connected via NMEA 0183 for position to be embedded in the distress signal. Verify GPS lock before any passage.**

1. Lift the red cover over the **DISTRESS** button
2. Hold the **DISTRESS** button for **5 seconds**
3. Radio transmits automated DSC distress signal on **Channel 70** with MMSI, position, and time
4. Radio auto-switches to **Channel 16** — monitor for Coast Guard / vessel response
5. Make Mayday voice call on Ch 16:
   > *"Mayday Mayday Mayday, this is S/V Agora, MMSI 368362150, [position], [nature of distress], [persons aboard], [assistance needed]"*

**To cancel accidental distress call:** Press CANCEL softkey immediately, then call USCG on Ch 16 to confirm cancellation.

## Important Operating Notes
- **MMSI is programmed** — do not attempt to re-program; Class D radios typically allow only one MMSI entry without a dealer reset
- **GPS link** — without NMEA GPS feed, distress calls transmit without position data. Significantly reduces rescue effectiveness
- **Channel 16 monitoring** — US law requires Ch 16 monitoring while underway. Use dual/tri-watch to monitor Ch 16 alongside a working channel
- **Weather** — NOAA WX channels (WX1–WX10) via the WX button
- **AquaQuake** — speaker water-clearing function; activates to vibrate water out of grille after spray
- **Hailer/PA** — external hailer speaker connector on rear panel

## Legal Requirement
Monitor Channel 16 at all times while underway (US law).
