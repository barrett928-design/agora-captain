# Safety Equipment

## Garmin inReach SE+ / Explorer+

### What it does
- Two-way satellite text messaging via Iridium network (global, works offshore)
- SOS rescue — contacts Garmin Response Center 24/7
- GPS tracking (family can follow on MapShare)
- Weather forecasts (basic, premium, or marine)

### SOS Procedure
1. Lift protective cap from SOS button
2. Press and hold SOS button
3. Wait for countdown — device sends location to emergency responders
4. Reply to confirmation message from Garmin Response
5. First 10 min: location update every minute
6. After 10 min: every 10 min moving, every 30 min stationary

### Key Notes
- Requires active Iridium satellite subscription (inreach.garmin.com)
- Needs clear view of sky to transmit
- Pair with phone via Earthmate app (Bluetooth) for larger screen use
- Battery: up to 100 hrs default; 30 days extended tracking mode
- Water rating: IPX7 (1m for 30 min)
- Rinse with fresh water after salt water exposure
- Test device before each offshore passage

- Check dates annually
