## Fluids

### Engine Oil
- **API Classification:** CD  
- **SAE Viscosity:** 15W40
- Do NOT use non-specified oils — causes seizure, abnormal wear, shortened engine life

### Marine Gear Oil (KM4A/KMH4A — Agora's gear)
- **API Classification:** CC or higher  
- **SAE Viscosity:** #20 or #30 (no multi-grade)

### Coolant
- Use LLC (long-life coolant / antifreeze) mixed with fresh water
- Follow manufacturer's mixing ratio for your temperature range
- Do NOT mix different brands of LLC
- Excessive LLC concentration reduces cooling efficiency

## Pre-Start Checks (Daily)

1. Check engine oil level (dipstick — between upper and lower marks)
2. Check cooling water level in **subtank** (fill to upper mark with fresh water if low)  
   - Do NOT remove fresh water tank cap while engine is hot — steam will scald you
   - Subtank level rises during operation (normal) — drops back when engine cools
3. Check marine gear oil level
4. Check seawater outlet flow
5. Check fuel level — replenish if low
6. Check alarm lamps function (all should illuminate at key-ON)
7. Check remote control handle moves smoothly — lubricate cable if stiff

---

## Starting Procedure

### Daily Start (Normal Conditions)
1. Open Kingston cock (seacock for raw water — if applicable)
2. Open fuel tank cock
3. Put remote control handle in **NEUTRAL**
4. Turn battery switch ON
5. Insert key → turn to **ON** — alarm buzzer sounds and alarm lamps illuminate (confirming alarms work)
6. Turn key to **START** — release immediately when engine fires; key auto-returns to ON
7. Confirm alarm lamps go off and buzzer stops
8. Warm up at low rpm before engaging gear

### Cold Start (Below 0°C)
- After step 6: Turn key to **GLOW** and hold to heat air heater
- Do NOT hold GLOW for more than **20 seconds** — will cause damage
- Turn key to START and start engine

### If Engine Fails to Start
- Wait at least **15 seconds** between start attempts — do not hold starter on more than 15 seconds at a time
- Air in fuel system = most common cause; bleed the fuel system
- Before restarting, confirm engine has **completely stopped** — restarting while turning damages starter pinion

## Oil Change Procedure

1. Drain oil while engine is still warm (oil flows better)
2. Attach oil drain pump hose to dipstick guide tube
3. Remove and replace oil filter at same time:
   - Turn filter left with filter wrench to remove
   - Clean filter mounting face
   - Apply oil to new filter gasket
   - Install by hand until gasket contacts seat, then tighten **3/4 turn** with filter wrench
   - Torque: 19.6–23.5 N·m (2.0–2.4 kgf·m)
4. Fill with new oil to upper mark on dipstick
5. Run engine ~5 minutes; check for leaks
6. Stop engine, wait 10 minutes, recheck dipstick — add oil if needed

**Oil capacity reference:**
- Oilpan only: 5.2 L
- Total (including filter): 6.3 L
- Add ~0.5 L extra when changing (accounts for oil held in old filter)

## Fuel Filter Replacement (Every 250 hrs / 1 yr)

Part Number: **129574-55710**

1. Close fuel cock on tank
2. Drain fuel from filter drain cock
3. Disconnect wiring connector and alarm switch
4. Remove filter with filter wrench
5. Clean filter mounting face
6. Apply fuel to gasket of new filter
7. Install hand-tight until gasket seats, then tighten **3/4 turn** with wrench
   - Torque: 11.8–15.6 N·m (1.2–1.6 kgf·m)
8. Reconnect alarm switch wiring
9. Bleed the fuel system
10. Start engine and check for fuel leaks

---

## Turbocharger Blower Wash (Every 250 hrs / 1 yr)

Product: Blower Wash (4L), P/N **974500-00400**

1. Run engine at 2,500–3,000 rpm in neutral
2. Slowly pour ~50cc of blower wash into the air intake over ~10 seconds
3. After ~3 minutes, slowly pour ~50cc of **fresh water** in the same way
4. Run for ~10 minutes, then check boost pressure and power output
5. Repeat if no improvement; if still no improvement — consult Yanmar dealer

**CRITICAL:** Do NOT pour large amounts all at once — can cause water hammer in combustion chamber and damage blower blades.

---

## Seawater Pump Impeller

- Inspect at **1,000 hrs / 4 yrs** interval
- Replace every **2,000 hrs** (mandatory)
- Inspect sooner if discharge volume drops noticeably
- **Impeller direction:** shaft turns clockwise, but impeller blades must be installed **counterclockwise**
- If water leaks continuously during operation: mechanical seal replacement required — consult Yanmar dealer

---

## Stopping the Engine

1. Set throttle to low idle speed
2. Put gear in NEUTRAL
3. Stop engine with kill switch/lever
4. Close fuel cock (if fitted)
5. Close Kingston cock (seacock)

## Troubleshooting

### Alarm Lamp Reference

| Lamp | Probable Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Charge lamp | Faulty battery, loose/damaged V-belt, alternator failure | Check battery fluid; adjust V-belt; if belt OK → dealer |
| C.W. Temp lamp | Low coolant level, water leak, pump failure, dirty system | Check & replenish coolant; if coolant OK → dealer |
| L.O. Press. lamp | Low engine oil | Replenish engine oil immediately |
| Fuel Filter lamp | Water/dirt in fuel filter | Drain fuel filter |
| Fuel Oil lamp | Low fuel level | Replenish fuel |

**If ANY alarm lamp illuminates while underway:** Put clutch in neutral, reduce to low idle, identify the alarm lamp, stop engine, investigate. If uncertain — return to port at low speed.

### Starting Failures

| Problem | Probable Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Starter works but engine won't start | No fuel | Replenish; bleed system |
| | Air in fuel line | Bleed fuel system |
| | Bad/contaminated fuel | Replace with correct fuel |
| | Clogged fuel filter | Replace fuel filter |
| | Poor fuel injection | Dealer service |
| Starter won't turn or slow | Dead/low battery | Check battery charge |
| | Bad cable connection | Clean terminals, retighten |
| | Faulty starter switch or safety switch | Dealer service |

### Exhaust Smoke

| Color | Probable Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Black smoke | Overload, bad fuel, low boost pressure, dirty turbo blower, excessive valve clearance | Reduce load; wash turbo blower; check fuel; dealer for valve clearance |
| White smoke | Bad fuel, injection timing delay, oil burning | Check fuel; dealer service |

### Clutch/Gear Problems

| Problem | Probable Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Remote control handle stiff | Rusty cable | Grease or replace cable |
| Propeller turns in NEUTRAL | Clutch lever misaligned, prop shaft incorrectly installed | Adjust clutch lever; dealer |
| No propulsion in FORWARD or REVERSE | Trawling lever in wrong position, low clutch oil, broken oil pump, worn clutch plate | Move trawling lever to H; refill clutch oil; dealer |

## Emergency Marine Gear Repair (KMH4A only)

If marine gear fails completely and the boat cannot move, the emergency bolt can lock the clutch to engine to allow **FORWARD ONLY** movement to return to port.

**CRITICAL WARNINGS:**
- Forward motion only — NO neutral, NO reverse
- Operate at **1,000 rpm or less only**
- Do NOT use clutch while emergency bolt is engaged — will break the gear
- Get to Yanmar dealer immediately after

**Procedure:**
1. Turn off engine
2. Remove red hexagonal bolts at back of marine gear
3. If emergency bolt is not visible: use turning stick in holes on front of V-pulley, rotate in direction of crankshaft rotation until emergency bolt becomes visible
4. Insert emergency bolt tightening tool; tighten bolt — **torque: 18.6±1.0 N·m (1.9±0.1 kgf·m)**
5. Repeat for second bolt (alternate torque evenly)
6. Replace hexagonal bolt
7. Fill marine gear oil to filler port mouth
8. Start engine with handle in neutral; proceed at low speed (1,000 rpm max)

## Long-Term Storage

1. Drain cooling water from all 4 positions: cylinder block side, fresh water pump, turbocharger, exhaust manifold
2. Clean outside of engine; wipe off dust and oil
3. To prevent condensation: either drain fuel tank or fill it completely
4. Grease exposed joints of remote control cable and bearings
5. Cover intake silencer, exhaust pipe with vinyl to prevent moisture entry
6. Drain bilge completely
7. Turn off battery switch; charge battery once per month during storage
