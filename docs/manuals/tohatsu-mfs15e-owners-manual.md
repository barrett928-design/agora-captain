## Fluids

### Engine Oil
- **Grade:** API SF–SM (or "FCW" designation)
- **Viscosity:** SAE 10W-30
- **Capacity:** ~1,000 mL
- Do NOT overfill — check dipstick

### Gear Oil (Lower Unit)
- **Grade:** API GL-5 or Tohatsu Genuine Gear Oil
- **Viscosity:** SAE #80-90
- **Capacity:** ~370–465 mL — drain and refill; stop filling when oil reaches lower drain plug hole
- Oil plug gasket: **metal shim only** — the correct replacement gasket is still needed for Agora's unit; monitor for leaks after oil change

## Break-In Period (New / Rebuilt Engine)

- First 10 hours: do not exceed 3/4 throttle
- Vary throttle speed — avoid prolonged constant RPM
- First oil change: after break-in (10 hrs)
- After break-in: full throttle permitted

---

## Pre-Start Checks

1. Check engine oil level (with motor vertical)
2. Check gear oil level if any signs of leak
3. Check fuel level and fuel line connection
4. Confirm tell-tale (cooling water indicator) exits when engine runs
5. Confirm kill switch lanyard is attached

---

## Starting Procedure

### Cold Start (EFI — no choke needed)
1. Attach kill switch lanyard
2. Set gear to NEUTRAL
3. Squeeze fuel primer bulb until firm (if long storage)
4. Set throttle to START position
5. Pull starter cord or press electric start
6. Engine should start quickly — EFI handles cold enrichment

### Warm Start
- No primer bulb needed if recently run
- Throttle to idle, start normally

---

## Stopping
1. Return throttle to idle
2. Put gear in NEUTRAL
3. Press stop button (or pull kill switch)
4. Flush with fresh water after saltwater use

---

## Flush After Saltwater Use

Failure to flush accelerates corrosion of internal cooling passages:

1. Connect flush adapter or place lower unit in fresh water bucket
2. Start engine
3. Run at idle for **5 minutes minimum**
4. Stop engine, remove flush connection

---

## Gear Oil Change Procedure

1. Place outboard vertically (motor down)
2. Remove UPPER drain plug first (keep handy)
3. Remove LOWER drain plug — oil drains out
4. Install new copper/aluminum gasket on lower plug; reinstall lower plug hand-tight
5. Inject gear oil into lower plug hole until oil level reaches lower hole — then quickly reinstall lower plug
6. Reinstall upper vent plug
7. Run engine briefly; check for leaks at both plugs

**Note for Agora:** The correct metal shim gasket for the oil plug still needs to be sourced — monitor lower plug for weeping after any oil change.

---

## Engine Oil Change Procedure

1. Run engine briefly to warm oil
2. Place container under engine
3. Remove drain plug (bottom of engine)
4. Allow oil to drain fully
5. Reinstall drain plug with new gasket
6. Fill with fresh SAE 10W-30 to dipstick upper mark
7. Run 2 minutes, recheck level

---

## Winterization / Long-Term Storage

1. Flush cooling system with fresh water
2. Fog engine cylinders with fogging oil (via intake or spark plug holes)
3. Change engine oil (used oil contains acids that corrode)
4. Change gear oil (check for milky appearance = water intrusion)
5. Remove battery if electric start; charge and store
6. Store motor in upright position or horizontal per manual

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Probable Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No cooling water from tell-tale | Clogged or failed water pump impeller | Replace impeller |
| Hard to start when hot | Vapor lock | Wait 5 min; attempt restart at full choke-off |
| Engine won't start | Kill switch not connected | Reconnect lanyard |
| White smoke | Water in combustion chamber | Gear oil seal failure — inspect impeller and seals |
| Rough idle | Fouled spark plugs | Clean or replace |
| Loss of power | Air filter clogged | Clean/replace air filter |
